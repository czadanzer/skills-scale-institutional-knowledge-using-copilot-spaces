# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers research, design, and iterate on user experience flows and interfaces. They ensure solutions are intuitive, accessible, and delightful for end-users while meeting product and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design wireframes, prototypes, and final UI/UX solutions
- Collaborate with Product Managers to translate requirements into user-centered designs
- Work with Developers to ensure designs are feasible and pixel-perfect
- Document design systems and design decisions
- Advocate for user needs during trade-off discussions

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that are usable and accessible
- Reduce user friction and improve adoption
- Build design consistency and maintain design systems

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions with Product and Engineering
- User research findings and insights shared with stakeholders
- Design documentation and component libraries
- Participation in sprint planning and retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Align on user needs, prioritize design work, validate solutions with data
- **Developers**: Collaborate on implementation details, review feasibility, ensure design fidelity
- **Project Manager**: Estimate design timelines and dependencies

---

## Security Leads

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure that projects incorporate security best practices, comply with policies and regulations, and mitigate security risks throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, design, and implementation plans for security risks
- Educate teams on secure coding practices and threat models
- Conduct or coordinate security reviews and penetration testing
- Manage compliance requirements and audit documentation
- Liaise with the Security team and respond to security incidents
- Update threat models and security guidelines as needed

### Goals
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and compliance violations
- Build security awareness across teams
- Enable secure, compliant feature delivery

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and code review comments
- Threat modeling sessions and risk assessments
- Security incident reports and post-incident reviews
- Training and guidance for the broader team

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide secure coding guidance, review implementations, conduct threat modeling
- **Product Manager**: Align on security requirements and compliance needs
- **Project Manager**: Flag security risks in the risk register, escalate blockers

---

## Data Analysts

### Role Summary
Data Analysts define instrumentation strategies, track success metrics, and provide insights to guide product decisions. They bridge product goals with data-driven evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Partner with Product Managers to define key metrics and success criteria
- Design instrumentation and data collection strategies
- Build dashboards and reporting for monitoring feature usage and health
- Conduct analysis and provide actionable insights
- Help interpret test results and inform go/no-go decisions

### Goals
- Enable data-informed decision-making
- Ensure product changes are measured and understood
- Identify trends and opportunities for optimization

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and instrumentation planning sessions
- Dashboard reviews and metric reporting
- Analysis reports and insights shared with Product and Project teams
- Participation in release planning and retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Define metrics, validate hypotheses, inform roadmap prioritization
- **Developers**: Collaborate on instrumentation implementation and data quality
- **Project Manager**: Provide metrics for project health and release readiness

---

## Customer Support / Customer Success

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Success teams interface directly with end-users, gather feedback, and escalate learnings back to product and engineering. They ensure customers can effectively use products and report issues.

### Responsibilities
- Respond to customer questions and resolve issues
- Gather and document customer feedback and feature requests
- Identify patterns in support issues and escalate systemic problems
- Collaborate during feature releases to ensure support readiness
- Participate in incident response and provide customer-facing communication
- Feed customer insights into product requirements and quality testing

### Goals
- Deliver excellent customer experience
- Reduce support burden through product quality and clarity
- Provide early warning of product or quality issues

### Typical Communication
- Support ticket systems and customer communication channels
- Feedback summaries and issue escalations shared with Product and Engineering
- Pre-release preparation and launch coordination
- Incident response and post-mortem participation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager**: Report customer feedback, validate feature prioritization, identify user pain points
- **Developers**: Escalate critical bugs, help reproduce issues, provide customer context
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate on release readiness, support launch activities, communicate timelines

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Reference these definitions during project kickoff to ensure all team members understand roles, responsibilities, and interaction patterns.
